Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

7-Day Performance Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel, for that channel.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it uses two read-only Google permissions: View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) and View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content (yt-analytics.readonly). It reads statistics about the owner's own videos, such as views, watch time, retention, traffic sources and audience summaries. It cannot upload, edit, delete or manage anything on YouTube.

How the data is used and stored

The statistics are written to the owner's private Notion workspace and kept in a private file on the owner's own server, solely to review each video's performance. Nothing is published, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored statistics can be deleted by the channel owner at any time.
